Lilypad Leap

Snap flies mid-leap and land on a lily pad. Miss the pad and it's a splash.

  • Drag back anywhere and let go. The dotted arc is the exact path.
  • Gold rings mark flies the tongue will reach.
  • Green ring means a safe landing.
  • Red cross means a splash and a lost life.
  • Pad colours: green is safe. Yellow, orange and pink pads sink a few seconds after you land.
  • The bullfrog hops after you. If he reaches your pad, he gulps you. Golden flies daze him.

  • Drag back from anywhere and let go. The dotted arc is the exact path the frog will take.
  • Gold rings mark flies the tongue will reach, shown where the fly will be at that moment.
  • Green ring at the end means a safe landing on a pad.
  • Red cross means the frog drops into the pond and loses a life.
  • Four stages: Still Pond, Breeze, Dusk and Night Storm. Green pads are safe; yellow sinks 4s after you land, orange 2.5s, pink 1.5s.
  • The bullfrog wakes on your first leap and hops after you, sinking every pad he leaves. If he reaches your pad, he gulps you. Golden flies daze him for a moment.

Keyboard: ←→ angle, ↑↓ power, Space leap. Eat flies on consecutive leaps to build a streak; every 12 flies earns a lily pad life.
